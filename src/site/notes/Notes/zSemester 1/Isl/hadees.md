> 1. Hadith e **Qauli**: said by PBUH
> 2. Hadith e **Faili**: done by PBUH
> 3. Hadith e **Taqreeri**: approved by PBUH
> > [!NOTE] Based on authencity(in order)
> > 1. **Sahih:** reported by trusted person
> > 2. **Hasan:** reported by trusted person with weak memory
> > 3. **Da'if:** the chain of reporters is incomplete
