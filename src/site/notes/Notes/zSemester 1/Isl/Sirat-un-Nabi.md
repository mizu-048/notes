> [!NOTE]- Kindness:
> 
> 1.  Woman in taif used to throw garbage on him, despite this when she fell ill, he showed kindness to her and took care of her
>     
> - The Prophet advised his wife A’ishah to never turn away needy people empty-handed and emphasized loving and caring for the poor, highlighting kindness as a mark of faith and a path to nearness to God.    
> - whenever a poor tribe came ot medina he collected donations to help the tribe

> [!NOTE]- Behavior with Relatives:​
> - He maintained good relations with his relatives even if they did not accept Islam, helping them in times of need without mistreatment. like his uncle Abu talib who never coverted to islam but always supported him
> - He helped his daughter fatimah in her household, visiting often and supporting her family life

> [!NOTE]- Increasing Respect of Women:
> 
> - The Prophet elevated the status of women by honoring them, giving them rights such as inheritance and consent, and emphasizing their dignity at a time when women were largely oppressed. ​
> - He treated women with great kindness and politeness, specifically emphasizing equal honor and dignity to women in a society where they were otherwise mistreated.
> - He gave women proper rights in a society and forbid their infanticide and publicaly

> [!NOTE]- Treatment with Non-Muslims:
> 
> 1. The Prophet practiced justice and kindness to non-Muslims, listening to their arguments patiently, responding with good manners, and never forcing Islam upon them.
> - When a Jewish funeral procession passed, he showed respect to the deceased by standing up, teaching kindness and respect regardless of religion.
> - He always maintained peace with non-muslims and always aimed for mutual understanding and coexistence

> [!NOTE]- Good Behavior with Slaves
> 
> 1. The Prophet was kind and just toward slaves, treating them with compassion and encouraging the freeing of slaves as a virtuous act (general known fact from Islamic history).
>     
> 2. He himself engaged in humble work such as mending his clothes and milking goats, setting an example of humility and service regardless of social status.
> 3. He always preached good behavior with slaves, even going as far to say that one cannot forgive their slave for one of their action

> [!NOTE]- Equality:
> 
> - The Khutba Hijatulwida Prophet emphasized that all humans are equal regardless of race, color, tribe, or belief, promoting justice and human dignity universally.​
> - He always showed justice, not even forgiving a girl for thievery, one who had the same name as his daughter
> - He demonstrated this by forgiving enemies after the conquest of Mecca and encouraging Muslims to treat neighbors and others with kindness, irrespective of their background
