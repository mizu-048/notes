## non-uniform circular motion
- ![nonuniform-circular-motion.png|500](/img/user/Notes/zSemester%201/Physics/attachments/nonuniform-circular-motion.png)
- in non-uniform circular motion the acceleration is not necessarily directed at the center and various with the change in velocity
- Tangential acceleration:
$$a_{T}=\dfrac{d}{dt}v;\qquad \vec{a} =\vec{a_{r}}+\vec{a_{T}}$$
- Due to tangential acceleration the resulting tangential force/net force is not necessarily towards the center 
$$
\begin{align}
(F_{ \text{net})_{r}}= ma_{r}  & = \dfrac{mv^{2}_{t}}{r} =m \omega^{2} r; \qquad \text{toward center} \\
 \\
(F_{ \text{net})_{t}} & = ma_{t} ;\qquad \text{tangential}\\
 \\
(F_{ \text{net})_{z}} & = 0
\end{align}
$$
