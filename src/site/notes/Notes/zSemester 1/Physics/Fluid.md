**Intensive:** Independent of the amount of matter—stays the same whether you have a little or a lot (e.g., density, temperature.
**Intrinsic:** Inherent to the material's composition and structure—doesn't change with external conditions (often overlaps with intensive, like melting point, hardness.
**Extrinsic:** Depends on external factors or conditions—not inherent to the material itself (e.g., weight, color under certain lights).
**Extensive:** Depends on the amount of matter—scales with size (e.g., mass, volume, total energy).
