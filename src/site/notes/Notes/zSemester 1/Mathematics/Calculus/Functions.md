$\dfrac{40}{N}\times H=\dfrac{40}{\text{Teeth of gear}}\times \text{no. of holes in plate}$

if $\dfrac{40}{N}$ gives $0$ in remainder (no fractional value)

if its a fraction like say $N = 27$ then $\dfrac{40}{27} =1+\dfrac{13}{27}$
in this case the remainder is $13$ now we will multiply $13$ with the available holes (should be told or written on the indexing plate) and divide by $27$ until we get a whole number, this basically involves trial and error until we get a whole number
